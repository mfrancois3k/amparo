A shared starting point

What participants do.

  1. Choose a state and an available situation.
  2. Practice a response, at their own pace.
  3. Repeat until the words feel more familiar.

Core practice, English and Spanish, basic printable materials, and resource links remain free. No account is required to practice.

A facilitator does not need a participant roster or personal legal histories to share the practice link. Ask participants to choose what they feel comfortable practicing.
