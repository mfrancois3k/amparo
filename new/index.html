A few things worth knowing.

Is practice really free?

Yes. Core practice, English and Spanish, basic printable cards, and legal-aid links are free. Physical products and organization services are optional and currently at prelaunch or inquiry stage.

Is this legal advice?

No. Amparo is a practice and information tool, not a law firm. Independent attorney review is pending. Laws and circumstances differ; consult a qualified lawyer for advice about your situation.

What happens to my voice?

Amparo does not save a voice recording. If you choose speech recognition, your browser or device provider may process audio. You can use the non-voice practice options.

What is stored?

Practice progress and settings are stored in this browser. Optional card accounts store card text you choose to save. Error reports are used for diagnostics. Read the privacy summary for the details.

Can my organization use Amparo?

Groups can explore the proposed packages and request information. Pricing, physical materials, facilitation, and availability must be confirmed before any order.

Does practice guarantee a safe encounter?

No. Rehearsal can help make words familiar, but cannot control another person’s behavior or guarantee safety or a legal outcome.
