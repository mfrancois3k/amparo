# Amparo focus group — target-niche members

- **Rosa, 44** — GA, Spanish-first house cleaner, mixed-status family, two kids (son 17 drives). Wants: her son safe, everything in Spanish, nothing that collects data. Distrusts: mic/camera permissions, anything asking for accounts. Budget: tight; $19 is a real decision. Says yes when: someone she trusts (church, consulate, legal-aid org) vouches.
- **Marcus, 19** — NY, Black college student, new driver, got "the talk" from his dad. Wants: to feel ready, not lectured. Distrusts: apps wanting his voice/location. Budget: broke; $19 for himself = no. Says yes when: it's free, gifted, or under ~$5; shares things that look sharp.
- **Dana, 52** — TX suburb, mom of a 16-year-old about to solo drive. Wants: to DO something about her fear; runs drills with her kid. Distrusts: nothing much online, but hates flaky products. Budget: $19 is trivial vs $400 driving school. Says yes when: it looks legit and prints clean.
- **Luis, 27** — TX, DACA, warehouse shift lead, older Android, prepaid data. Wants: exact scripts, offline, zero traces. Distrusts: card payments creating identity trails, anything cloud. Budget: has $19, won't leave a payment record for THIS product. Says yes when: cash-like path or handed to him by a nonprofit.
- **Tony, 61** — GA, retired Black postal worker, gives "the talk" to grandkids himself. Wants: respect for lived experience. Distrusts: tech promising safety from paper ("a card won't stop a bad cop"). Budget: fine. Says yes when: community institutions he trusts (church, NAACP chapter) put their name on it.
