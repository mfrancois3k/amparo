Before you speak: physical safety

🚗 Pull over fully, engine off, hazards on at night
👐 Hands visible on the wheel — 10 and 2 — before the officer reaches you
💡 Interior light on at night; window down enough to talk
🗣 Announce every reach: “My license is in the glovebox — may I get it?”
🚪 Never step out unless ordered — then comply

One question before you practice

Are you currently on probation, parole, or supervised release? Your rights are different — many supervision conditions include standing consent to searches, so “I do not consent” and “am I free to go” can create real risk for you. You’ll see a standing supervision warning while you practice.

Everyone freezes. That's why we practice.

In a real stop, your heart rate doubles and words disappear. Three practiced sentences bring them back. Say them out loud here — where mistakes are free — so they're there when it counts.

Built by Michael Francois. Private by design — your practice never leaves this device.

How practice works

The officer speaks first. Pick a response — or type or say your own. Strong lines protect you; risky lines teach you why. Say every line OUT LOUD — that\u2019s what builds the reflex.

The steadiness bar

Under stress your voice shakes. The moving needle is your voice — answer while it\u2019s inside the gold zone to stay steady. It moves faster and the zone shrinks at higher difficulty and in ⚡ pressure mode.

Heart rate & the composure bonus

Your ❤️ BPM rises with risky answers and pressure mode, and calms with strong steady ones. Finish clean with a steady voice to EARN the ✨ composure bonus — it isn\u2019t given, it\u2019s earned.
