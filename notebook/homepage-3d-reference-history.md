## Reconstruction intent

The current request is to reconstruct this visual concept as a dimensional homepage experience. The intended three dioramas carry recognition at a traffic encounter, the distinction between inside and outside at a home threshold, and a composed response at a vehicle window. Their purpose is to introduce preparation and the site's practice and support tools.

The original six files and the existing photographic assets are retained as references. New visual interpretation does not authorize changes to legal dialogue, legal claims, or scenario availability. Any depiction of the door encounter must preserve the existing held door-practice review gate; a visual preview must not imply that the held practice scenario is available.

This document records reference provenance and design intent only. It does not certify generation, implementation, or testing.
