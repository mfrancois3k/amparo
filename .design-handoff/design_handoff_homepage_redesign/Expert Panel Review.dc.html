Expert panel review · 3 passes · zero bloat

Amparo homepage v2 — practice-first

Scope: the redesigned homepage, the live in-page Arena demo (shipped this pass), the path to a yes for 10 target niches — and a pass 3 on the endeavor itself (process, strategy, and this review's own method). Personas come from the repo's own focus-group file. Severity: P0 blocks buyers now · P1 costs conversions · P2 polish.

Panel verdicts — pass 1

Brand / Apple-style art directionB+

Hierarchy reads, cream/navy/gold is ownable, the hero verb is right. Biggest miss: placeholder imagery reads as vaporware — Dana's "flaky product" alarm.

Conversion strategyB−

Practice-first is the correct funnel; a playable demo beats claims. Missing: returning-user path, share loop, and any org/vouching layer — three of the live site's strongest assets.

Trust & legal-content UXA−

Disclaimers are placed honestly and the "lowers risk, can't remove it" line is an asset. Surface per-scenario attorney status ("Reviewed" / "In review") — honesty is this brand's moat.

Bilingual content (ES-first)B+

Passes the Marisol test so far — ES reads as written, not translated. «¿Soy libre de irme?» is the anchor phrase; never paraphrase it. Rule: no EN module ships without ES in the same commit.

AccessibilityC+

Gold-on-cream chips sit near the contrast line; the demo needs keyboard focus states, aria-live on feedback, and prefers-reduced-motion for the tension bar. Omar is blocked today.

Game / instructional designB

The demo ships the product's best mechanic — the admission branch ("Watch what happens"). Guard the emotional register: never confetti a scenario that ends in cuffs; keep swan levels unscored.

Growth & distributionC

Rosa and Tony don't convert from ads — they convert when a church, NAACP chapter, or legal-aid org puts its name on it. The product already sells org packs ($149/$499); the page never mentions them. Highest-ROI fix on this list.

Blindspots — all of them, with solutions

P0

No returning-user state. The live site's "Continue where you left off" is its best-performing button; the redesign forgets everyone who already started. Fix: localStorage flag flips the hero primary to "Continue where you left off."

P0

No vouching / organizations layer. Two of ten personas only buy on institutional trust, and org SKUs already exist in-product. Fix: an "Amparo for Organizations" band — bulk printed packs, facilitator guide, invoice-friendly, no member data collected.

P0

Simulation-first can repel stop survivors (Nia). A guns-drawn trainer as the front door is the exact failure the root product's consent gates exist to prevent. Fix (partly shipped): demo is opt-in behind a tap; "Gentle mode — no clock or heartbeat" now a visible chip; keep the pack as a clearly-labeled non-simulation route.

P0

Monetization opacity. The page says "free" everywhere while the Arena upsells a $3.99 Script Pack after a win. That gap is where trust dies. Fix: one honest line — "Practice is free. An optional $3.99 script pack exists. No surprise paywall."

P0

Demo corpus is not attorney-reviewed yet. The Arena's 24 scenarios are marked "in the review pile" in the repo. Marketing it without saying so betrays the brand's one rule. Fix (shipped): "in legal review" stated in the demo caption; add the ⚖ practice-tool marker verbatim when scenarios embed deeper.

P1

No share loop. "Send this to someone you love" is the live site's growth engine — WhatsApp cards for it already exist in EN/ES. Fix: share strip with prefilled WhatsApp/SMS; it's how Rosa reaches her son and Dana reaches Devin.

P1

State framing reads as exclusion (Ana). "TX, GA and NY have their own statutes" tells an Arizona driver this isn't for her. Fix: flip the frame — "Works in all 50 states. TX/GA/NY add statute-level citations." Federal-only states framed by what they cover.

P1

Print-centric framing excludes no-printer users (Keisha). Highest need, lowest patience, zero printers between fares. Fix: "No printer? Everything works on-screen — print later at a library." Plus a 30-second quick-drill entry.

P1

Mic-permission fear (Luis, Marcus). "Voice answers" as a headline feature reads as "this app wants my voice." Fix (shipped): "Answer by tap, voice, or text" chip + "No mic, no account" on the demo start screen. Never request the permission on page load.

P1

Passengers and non-drivers are invisible (Wes, Marisol). The Arena has full passenger scenarios; the page is driver-shaped. Fix (partly shipped): "Passengers get scenarios too" chip; consider a fourth scenario card.

P1

Accessibility debt. Contrast on gold chips, no focus rings, tension-bar motion, emoji inside read-aloud script lines. Fix: token pass on contrast, :focus-visible styles, prefers-reduced-motion, keep emoji out of screen-reader script text.

P2

No teen-gift path (Dana → Devin). The buyer and the end user are different people. Fix: a share link that opens directly into a scenario — no wizard, no lecture, no parent-coded framing.

P2

Placeholder imagery. Every trust-sensitive persona reads unfinished visuals as unfinished legal content. Fix: photograph the real printed pack + real Arena screenshots; og.png works as a stopgap.

P2

Offline story untold. Prepaid-data users (Luis, Keisha) need "works with no signal once loaded" said out loud. Fix: one line near privacy chips; the service worker already exists.

Pass 2 — the panel re-reviews its own fixes

Assume every fix above ships. What still fails:

1 · Legal debt compounds. Every new surface (demo, share cards, state picker) adds sentences that need attorney review. One review queue, every sentence in it, status visible — or the honesty positioning collapses.

2 · Re-bloat risk. Orgs band + share strip + state picker + demo is four new modules on a page praised for restraint. Hero keeps exactly two CTAs; everything new lives below the fold or dies.

3 · Don't market what's broken. The Arena's free-text matcher currently praises unsafe answers on comply-only turns (known P0 in the repo). Until fixed, the page must not advertise typed/voice answer grading.

4 · ES parity is a release gate, not a task. Any module that ships EN-only breaks the Marisol test and the brand promise in the same moment.

5 · No metrics, no verdict. Define demo-start → demo-finish → arena-open → pack-built events before launch, or pass 3 will be opinions again.

Pass 3 — blindspots of the whole endeavor

Gaps outside the page itself — in the process, the strategy, and this review's own method.

⚖ P0

The demo IS legal content — attorney review before launch, not after. The homepage now gives specific scripted answers and grades them; that is closer to advice than the pack ever was. A caption disclaimer may not cover it, and "in legal review" on a public page is an admission that unreviewed legal content is live — counsel may say that's worse than not shipping the demo. If only one item on this page happens: this one. Everything else is recoverable.

P0

State-blind scripts on a state-proud site. The demo's answers are correct generally, but some states impose duties (e.g. identifying yourself) where "I choose to remain silent" applied verbatim could make things worse — while the site's pitch is statute-level state accuracy. Fix: attorney pass per line; add "rules differ by state" beside the demo, or gate line variants on the state picker.

P0

Demo–Arena expectation gap. The demo funnels to /arena; if the real Arena's first 60 seconds feel worse (mic prompt, no tap mode, slower start), the homepage becomes a bait-and-switch. Fix: play both back-to-back before launch; the Arena's first minute must match or beat the demo.

P1

Redesigning blind to real analytics. Nobody diffed this against what the live page actually converts on. Fix: check current funnel data first; if "Continue where you left off" really is the best button, confirm before replacing the page around it.

P1

No rollback plan. A one-commit overwrite of index.html means any regression is a scramble. Fix: ship at /new or behind a flag; keep the old page one revert away.

P1

This panel is synthetic. Ten fictional personas — me role-playing your users — directionally useful, zero real evidence. Fix: five hallway tests with actual Spanish-first users beat all ten verdicts. Show the demo, watch where they hesitate.

P1

No acquisition story. Everything assumes people arrive at the homepage; nothing explains how they find it. Fix: the Organizations band is the acquisition strategy — and it's a sales motion (calls, pilots, printed samples), not a webpage.

P2

Words aren't the only variable. The demo rewards a calm, confident register; real stops go badly for people whose affect reads as nervous regardless of wording. Fix: marketing must never imply the script is the whole game — Tony's objection, applied to the demo itself. The "lowers risk, can't remove it" line belongs next to the demo too.
